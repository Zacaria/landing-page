## Variant: Editorial authority

### Design stance

A calm, senior, editorial identity that puts judgment and business-facing credibility ahead of technical spectacle.

### Key choices

- Layout: asymmetric hero, horizontal evidence ledger, editorial case-study rows.
- Typography: Newsreader for reflective authority; Manrope and DM Mono for clarity.
- Color: warm paper, dark green ink, restrained orange accents.
- Interaction: offset button hovers, case-study row movement, responsive navigation.

### Trade-offs

- Strong at: trust, strategic leadership, long-form readability, distinctive senior presence.
- Weak at: immediate “deep technologist” signaling; deliberately quiet rather than energetic.

### Best for

- CTOs, founders, and hiring leaders who need to see strategic judgment and mature communication first.
