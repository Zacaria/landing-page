## Variant: Systems signal

### Design stance

A dark, precise identity that presents Zacaria as the person called when a technical system is under pressure and the team needs clarity.

### Key choices

- Layout: structured grid, technical side rail, dense outcome cells, system-like case cards.
- Typography: Space Grotesk with IBM Plex Mono labels.
- Color: near-black, acid green, and signal blue.
- Interaction: responsive navigation, animated evidence bars, hover elevation, expandable leadership principles.

### Trade-offs

- Strong at: technical authority, API/platform credibility, measurable outcomes, memorable contrast.
- Weak at: can read closer to a developer-tool brand than a person; human leadership needs careful balance.

### Best for

- Engineering executives, platform teams, and technical founders evaluating deep architecture and operational competence.
