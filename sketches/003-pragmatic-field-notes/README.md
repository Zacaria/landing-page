## Variant: Pragmatic field notes

### Design stance

A warm, energetic personal identity that makes curiosity, communication, and team leadership visible without losing technical proof.

### Key choices

- Layout: illustrated asymmetric hero, outcome ribbon, irregular case-study composition, notebook-like leadership blocks.
- Typography: Literata for personality, DM Sans and IBM Plex Mono for practical clarity.
- Color: cream, navy, red-orange, sky blue, yellow, and soft green.
- Interaction: responsive navigation, tactile card and button movement, working anchors.

### Trade-offs

- Strong at: personality, approachability, memorable human detail, team and communication story.
- Weak at: less traditionally executive; playful geometry must be restrained in production.

### Best for

- Founders and product-minded engineering organizations looking for a strategic lead who is technically broad and easy to work with.
